# Contributing Guidelines
Anybody is welcome to contribute to the repository.

## AI Policy
LLM generated code is allowed, with strict rules towards its usage:

- AI is not allowed in PRs, Issues, and/or to be used in conversation within the repository.
- Low effort, recklessly vibecoded commits may not be accepted.
- You should be able to explain in your own words, without the use of an LLM, what your commit changes, and why.
